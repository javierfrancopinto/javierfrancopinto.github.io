Instituto de Economía, Facultad de Ciencias Económicas y Administración, Universidad de la República, Montevideo, Uruguay

I study what job loss does to household debt and credit, how sick-leave policy shapes presenteeism, and who bears the cost of fiscal consolidations, using Uruguayan administrative records and cross-country macro data.

Fields: macroeconomics, public and fiscal policy, inequality and poverty, household finance.

Research

I work on three lines of research at the Institute of Economics, FCEA. In inequality and poverty, I look at what happens to household debt and access to credit when the household head loses their job. With the labor economics group, I recently started working on contagious presenteeism in Uruguayan firms. In macroeconomics and fiscal policy, I study the distributional effects of fiscal consolidations, which is the topic of my master's thesis.

Interest over Interests: Analysis of Uruguayan Household Debt

Research assistant project, CSIC grant. Ongoing.

Follows household debt and access to credit after the household head loses their job, using individual-level administrative data, to see whether the financial system cushions the income shock or makes it worse.

Contagious Presenteeism in Uruguayan Firms

Research assistant project. Ongoing.

How public sick-leave subsidies interact with firm practices that discourage absenteeism, and whether workers end up coming in sick and infecting their coworkers. Uses labor-history and Sickness Subsidy records.

Does Austerity Redistribute?

Master's thesis. Expected defense November 2026.

A pooled panel of OECD and Latin American and Caribbean economies, with consolidation episodes dated using the narrative approach and effects estimated with local projections. I ask whether the distributional effects of fiscal consolidations depend more on a country's quality of government (a composite measure of state capacity) than on its region, and whether tax-based and expenditure-based adjustments still differ once state capacity is taken into account.
